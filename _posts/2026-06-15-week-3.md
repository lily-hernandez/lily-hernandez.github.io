During the third week, I used Python to calculate statistical features for each physiological signal type under both pain and no-pain conditions.

For each of the eight signal groups, I calculated five statistical values:

- Mean
- Maximum
- Minimum
- Variance
- Median

Because there were four signal types for the pain condition and four signal types for the no-pain condition, this process produced eight sets of statistical features.

These measurements summarize important characteristics of each signal. The mean and median describe the typical signal value, while the maximum and minimum show the overall range. Variance measures how much the signal values change or spread out over time.

The statistical feature table will also be useful later in the project because these values can serve as inputs for a machine-learning model that classifies pain versus no pain.
