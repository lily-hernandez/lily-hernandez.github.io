---

During the second week, I used Python to visualize the physiological signals contained in the PainData dataset. The goal was to create eight plots representing the different signal types under both pain and no-pain conditions.

I selected one example from each of the four physiological signal types for the pain condition and one example from each signal type for the no-pain condition. This resulted in four pain plots and four no-pain plots.

The visualizations made it possible to compare the shape, range, and variability of the signals under the two conditions. Some signals appeared relatively stable, while others showed larger fluctuations over time.

This step helped me become more familiar with the structure of each physiological signal and provided an initial visual understanding of how the signals may differ when a participant experiences pain.
